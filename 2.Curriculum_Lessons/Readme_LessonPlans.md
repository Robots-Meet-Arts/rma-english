# Lesson Plans

**29 ready-to-use activities for primary school teachers**

---

## Contents

This folder contains 8 introductory lessons and 21 subject-specific lessons across 7 humanities disciplines. Each subject area includes three lessons: one unplugged activity, one using block-based coding, and one featuring a floor robot.

## Browse by Category

**IntroductoryLessonPlans**  
Foundational activities to introduce robotics and computational thinking. Unplugged activities are screen-free; plugged activities use block-programming (MakeCode Arcade), OctoStudio and basic robotics.

**LessonPlansByComputingPractice**  
Activities organised by technology type: unplugged (no technology), block-based programming, or robotics.

**LessonPlansByDisciplines**  
Subject-specific lessons integrating robotics with humanities content: Arts, Civic Education, Foreign Language, Geography, History, Mother Language, and Music.

---

## Lesson Plan Format

Each lesson plan (PDF) includes:

- Quick reference panel (subject, age, duration, difficulty, group size)
- Learning objectives (subject-specific and computational thinking skills)
- Materials needed (equipment, printables, helpful links)
- Lesson procedure (Warm Up, Build Up, Rehearsal, Clean Up)
- Teacher notes (preparation, differentiation, classroom management)
- Annexes (printable worksheets, grids, movement cards)
